# Frank Miller Noir Visual Concepts — One Roof

These concepts explore a **Frank Miller graphic novel aesthetic** (*Sin City*, *The Dark Knight Returns*, *Hard Boiled*) for One Roof's 2.5D architectural cutaways and environment props.

Per `Docs/05_ASSET_PIPELINE.md`, these are **art-direction concepts** and live outside `Assets/`. They are not runtime assets until sliced, normalized, and validated through the asset compiler gate.

---

## Visual Direction Principles

1. **Extreme Chiaroscuro & Silhouette Primacy**:
   - Deep, pitch-black ink pools juxtaposed with razor-sharp white highlights.
   - Strong silhouettes ensure immediate room identification even at zoomed-out macro tower scale.
   - Harsh angular lighting cuts (e.g. venetian blind zebra striping, overhead industrial spot cones).

2. **Selective Spot Color Accents**:
   - Predominantly monochrome ink drawings with deliberate single-hue accents.
   - *Amber/Yellow*: Warmth in residential quarters (desk lamps), or hazard/warning stripes in utility shafts and power generators.
   - *Cyan/Teal*: CRT monitor glow, status terminals, cold computational readouts.
   - *Crimson/Red*: Alert beacons, lockdown indicators, restricted access stamps.

3. **Graphic Novel Texturing**:
   - Halftone dot screens (Ben-Day dots), coarse hatching, and ink splatter to convey grit, wear, and atmospheric tension without muddying geometric clarity.

## Concept Catalog

### 1. Rooms

- **`concept_room_noir_apartment.jpg`**:
  - *Type*: Tenement Studio / Micro-Apartment cutaway.
  - *Key Motifs*: Sharp venetian blind shadows across the concrete floor slab; fold-down Murphy bed mechanism; compact kitchenette with exposed drain piping; cast-iron radiator; rainy dystopian skyscraper backdrop through a cutaway window; warm amber desk lamp spot accent.
  - *Design intent*: Shows how living quarters can convey personal vulnerability and economic strain without becoming cartoonish misery.

- **`concept_room_security_hub.jpg`**:
  - *Type*: Security Station / Tower Surveillance C3 Level.
  - *Key Motifs*: Bank of glowing CRT surveillance monitors; heavy reinforced blast door with viewport hatch; stepped structural concrete bunker walls; trench-coated guard silhouette under a harsh conical lamp; spot accents of cyan terminal flicker and red lock status.
  - *Design intent*: Exemplifies the Steward's reach and scrutiny systems in a visually arresting surveillance chamber.

- **`concept_room_undercity_generator.jpg`**:
  - *Type*: Secret Undercity / Subterranean Fission-Thermal Generator.
  - *Key Motifs*: Raw excavated bedrock walls; massive turbine with braided conduit bundles and steam piping; suspended steel grated catwalks; mechanic silhouette holding an inspection trouble-light with selective yellow spotlight glow; diagonal hazard striping.
  - *Design intent*: Bridges the tower simulation with the subterranean expansion mechanic (excavation and secret power).
