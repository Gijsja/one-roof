# Game Vision

## One sentence

Build one enormous mixed-use tower, then live with the society that forms inside it.

## Format

- Genre: vertical-city builder and emergent society simulation.
- Presentation: controlled 2.5D architectural cutaway with modular 2D characters.
- Audience: players of city builders and colony simulations who enjoy legible cause and effect.
- Initial release: premium single-player desktop game.

## Player role

The player is the tower's Steward: developer, mayor, and systems designer. They build structure and services, lease space, set policy, inspect outcomes, and respond to crises. They cannot directly order an NPC to perform an action.

## Core loop

1. Build space and infrastructure.
2. Populate it through housing and business demand.
3. Observe daily life.
4. Diagnose spatial and social problems.
5. Respond through building, staffing, policy, or leasing.
6. Watch the population adapt.

## Product pillars

1. **Height is geography.** Elevators are streets and transfer lobbies are intersections.
2. **Architecture produces society.** Contact, access, cost, and shared grievances shape relationships and factions.
3. **Every major problem is explainable.** The player can move from symptom to cause to intervention.
4. **Scale without micromanagement.** Blueprints, zoning, leasing, and aggregated simulation let one tower grow into a city.
5. **People remain personal.** Inspectable lives and local relationship networks make aggregate systems emotionally legible.

## First playable

Five floors, fifty residents, apartments, offices, one shop, utilities, stairs, and elevators. Home/work/food trips create congestion. The elevator overlay explains the problem. Added capacity improves commute time and satisfaction.

## Beta boundary

- 30 floors and 300 persistent residents.
- Residential, office, retail, restaurant, clinic, maintenance, security, utility, and lobby rooms.
- Passenger transit, power, water, waste, and maintenance.
- Households, workplaces, friendships, grievances, and four faction archetypes.
- Eight overlays and six event chains.
- One campaign goal: sustain City Status.

Deferred: multiplayer, multiple towers, street simulation, detailed crime/politics, full furniture editing, mobile/console, and user-facing generation.
