# Architecture Decision Log

This log records active, governing architecture decisions.
For settled historical decisions (ADR-001 through ADR-069), see [`Docs/Archive/ADR_HISTORICAL_ARCHIVE.md`](./Archive/ADR_HISTORICAL_ARCHIVE.md).
Append new decisions; do not silently rewrite history. Use `Handoffs/DECISION_TEMPLATE.md` for full records when needed.

| ID | Status | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ADR-070 | Accepted | Construct Data-mode explanations in one Application module | Seven independent overlay builders multiplied wiring in the Tower coordinator and spread the symptom-to-cause contract across files. `TowerDataOverlays` owns typed, lazy projections for transit, residents, Scrutiny, businesses, and utilities; the elevator projection is cached by congestion identity so HUD and inspector reads reuse it. |
| ADR-071 | Accepted | Model Outside as a first-class world endpoint | Residents currently begin in assigned rooms and trips require room IDs. Define `Outside` as a persistent Domain location connected to the tower through the ground-floor lobby entrance; move-ins and external commutes use this boundary, with a compact street-edge node as the initial world representation and street view/horizon as Presentation. Never encode Outside as an invalid room ID. |
| ADR-072 | Accepted | Add typed endpoint fields to the existing save DTOs | OR-1004 adds `WorldLocation` for residents and trips, with the street-edge graph node connected only to the lobby portal. Save DTOs add explicit location-kind fields and retain room ID fields for room endpoints; missing kinds from older saves default to Room. Active routes are rebuilt from typed endpoints on load, so graph node IDs are not persisted. Demand-created residents begin Outside, and a missing internal workplace becomes an external job rather than a home-as-work shortcut. |
| ADR-073 | Accepted | Present Outside as layered procedural cutaway scenery | The tower uses unlit quads, so `OutsideCityPresenter` generates three collider-free silhouette layers, irregular window grids, roof details, and lamps against the camera background instead of introducing unmanaged source art. The skyline stays near the lobby's visual scale, avoiding a hard-edged sky rectangle. Horizontal camera pans shift only three roots at depth-specific rates; facade and window colour changes use half-hour buckets. The overview camera shifts right to leave a visible city slice while keeping the tower in frame. No skyline object enters simulation or save data. |
| ADR-074 | Accepted | Steward UI shell and theme standardization | Replaces debug dashboard with compact steward status card, categorizes build palette into four groups (Space & Use, Services, Utilities, Movement & Removal), unifies mode dock (Build, Inspect, Data, Manage) under StewardTheme, and enables 7 selectable overlays in Data mode without direct simulation mutation. |
| ADR-075 | Accepted | Frame the Steward as an accountable building manager and make City Status inspectable | The player manages a single inhabited building through systems, leases, infrastructure, and decrees. This War of Mine informs human stakes, Fallout Shelter informs cutaway clarity and expansion, and Observer informs environmental investigation; none adds combat or direct resident control. The Beta campaign evaluates explicit daily wellbeing, service, fiscal, scale, and scrutiny gates for 30 consecutive days, with a visible cause for lost progress. |
| ADR-076 | Accepted | Give resident activities persistent minimum durations and explicit purpose | The coarse schedule remains an intent source, but each arrival begins a person-owned purpose interval: outside work holds for 480 ticks after arrival, and standard-schedule diner meals and home activities hold for 30 and 60 ticks. The compressed five-floor acceptance fixture uses 5-tick room episodes so its short morning commute remains testable. Urgent needs and schedule changes are reconsidered after the interval; the original schedule blocks and absolute interval survive save/load. This narrowly supersedes ADR-067's stateless mid-block assumption while preserving the pure Domain boundary. Presentation reads purpose and trip intent through projections, and hides residents located Outside. |
| ADR-077 | Accepted | Evaluate bounded social state and civil actions at daily settlement | Relationship edges have a six-edge resident cap and stable-ID tie breaks; faction support derives from existing wellbeing, employment, arrears, and policy signals. Daily settlement updates faction pressure and a deterministic warning/action/recovery/cooldown state machine. Save DTOs persist authoritative state; overlays and the bounded decision timeline consume immutable projections. Scrutiny modulates action duration only after faction eligibility, and the Steward retains systems-level control. |
| ADR-078 | Accepted | Keep underground earth excavation separate from tower floors and slabs | Earth beneath the building uses its own fixed 16×6, saveable 1 m grid rather than negative tower floors or slab bounds, so excavation does not alter resident routing or floor counts. Build mode supports square 1×1, 2×2, and 3×3 dig and lair-floor brushes with continuous click-drag interaction; lair floors require excavated cells and the cutaway renders earth, cavities, and floors from immutable projections. |
| ADR-079 | Accepted | Expand excavation into a connected secret undercity | Supersedes ADR-078's 16×6 size and no-routing boundary: the independent board is 32×12, with old cells centered during migration. A lobby-linked access core, shaft, corridors, and rectangular room zones form a separate underground route graph. Fourteen room types have distinct operating effects. Resident staffing is autonomous; outside investigators travel visible noncombat routes and cause recoverable disruption. Treasury and outside-market transfers stay explicit, and discovery feeds existing Scrutiny rather than adding a separate campaign failure state. |
| ADR-080 | Accepted | Fund and cap business output by paid work | New commercial tenants receive 500 units of outside opening capital; walk-in payroll covers only demand-supported active staff, and unfunded staff generate no customer or contract revenue. Diner tickets rise to 10 with 5/cell rent; workshop/security contract rates rise to 60/65 so full rooms can cover baseline costs. Outside wages rise to 24 so a working resident can cover basic rent and a meal. Leasing excludes `workplace:` rooms without a business ledger. Opening capital is an explicit source in conservation proofs; all rates remain deterministic domain defaults. |
| ADR-081 | Accepted | Recover business activity only when margins support it | Outside essential food spends cash without subtracting from the tower walk-in allowance. Leasing matches residents to funded productive positions and can refill those positions from existing Outside workers. Insolvent tenants stop operating after seven days; a new tenant needs a nonnegative projected daily margin under current demand and policy. Replacement capital and debt write-off remain explicit, persisted cash sources. This allows a temporary shock to recover while persistent losses leave vacancies rather than generating repeated recapitalizations. |
| ADR-082 | Accepted | Treat co-presence as a neutral social encounter | At daily social evaluation, a sampled shared room or route refreshes contact without changing affinity. Two consecutive days with the same named active grievance can strengthen the tie by 0.04; absent reinforcement, affinity returns toward neutral by 0.01 per day. Seven candidates per context are selected by a stable day/key/resident hash before the existing six-edge cap. Social save data version 2 persists the shared-support streak and last meaningful tick; legacy edges retain affinity but their cause is labeled unavailable. Conflict-driven changes wait for an attributable resident-to-resident event rather than inferring animosity from faction membership. |
| ADR-083 | Accepted | Integrate M10.1 building with explicit surface-to-undercity utility paths | Defer floor blueprint copy/paste. Keep the underground board independent, but derive power/water connectivity from built graph edges across an explicit lobby/core bridge. Presentation animates flowing paths and keeps disconnected built segments visible for diagnosis. The generator retains its existing scalar backup contribution until a reverse-feed contract is implemented; underground production likewise retains its current staffing/supply rules. Geometry refresh follows topology/service changes, with 30-floor/300-resident performance still requiring measurement. See `Planning/M10_1_CONNECTED_BUILDING_AND_UNDERCITY.md`. |
| ADR-084 | Accepted | Author the 30-floor playground as a deterministic seed plus a separate decorative environment prefab | The game uses normal simulation, projections, room furnishings, overlays and commands. Scene bootstrap activates an inactive controller in GoldStandardCity mode to avoid saving generated view hierarchies. Skyline/street meshes and bounded ambient actors are generated through Unity AssetDatabase and share two URP materials; they own no resident, collision or save state. City Status and hardware budgets remain independent acceptance gates. |
| ADR-085 | Accepted | Adopt 3-tier v0.10 documentation architecture and context shield | The v0.10 prototype has matured to 765 passing tests, but ~326 KB of legacy docs drained agent context windows. Establish a 3-tier structure: Tier 1 (canonical `AGENTS.md` <100 lines + 3-line pointer files), Tier 2 (authoritative runtime specs in `Docs/`), Tier 3 (strictly 1 active handoff in `Handoffs/Active/`, synchronized `BACKLOG.md`, archived planning briefs). Historical ADRs 001–069 archived to `Docs/Archive/ADR_HISTORICAL_ARCHIVE.md`. |
| ADR-086 | Accepted | Resolve stair navigation, shader passes, and GPU instancing for 30-floor scale | (1) Added short-hop stair routing and elevator queue diversion (`WaitTicks >= 15`, queue length >= 3) to eliminate elevator starvation. (2) Stripped `OneRoofUnlit.shader` to a single `SRPDefaultUnlit` pass (100% SRP batcher compatibility) and enabled GPU instancing on NPC materials, dropping draw calls from 14,585 to 171 (-98.8%) and boosting frame rate to ~108 FPS at 30 floors / 300 residents. |
| ADR-087 | Accepted | Implement Sims-style motives, RimWorld thoughts, and full emotional relationship sandbox | Extends `PersonRecord` and `FactionState` with explicit social traits (`HopelessRomantic`, `Charismatic`, `Flirt`, `HotHeaded`, `GrudgeHolder`, `Abrasive`, `Loyal`, `Jealous`), bounded thought memory buffers with tick expiry and mood delta, and a 12-tier relationship progression spectrum (`Stranger` through `Married` and `Nemesis`). `ResidentSocialSystem` evaluates co-presence during leisure/idle periods with deterministic chemistry rolls, generating emergent romance, rivalries, and attributable thought memories while strictly preserving pure-C# domain boundaries and `MaxEdgesPerResident = 6`. |
