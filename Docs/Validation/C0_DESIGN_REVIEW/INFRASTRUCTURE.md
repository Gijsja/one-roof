## Decisive source findings

1. **Contamination as drafted cannot occur on an operational supplying riser.** `Domain/Infrastructure/WaterWasteNetworkState.cs` calls IsOperational while finding every continuous column; it rejects condition<=.30 through UtilityOperationsSnapshot.IsFailed. The draft requires precisely that condition for a live backflow source. A physically failed riser can retain contaminated residue but cannot simultaneously be the current flowing source. Change the source condition, or explicitly create residual contamination on a disconnected asset and separately activate upon later reconnection. Do not claim the draft already produces unsafe full-pressure water.
2. **Repair-to-.50 is insufficient for proposed 120+240 healthy recovery.** At wear .006/tick, .50 falls below .40 in about17 ticks and fails in about34. Current one-technician repair oscillates around .30; the alternative numerical model confirms periodic repeat failures with a repair-to-.50 latch. Preventive maintenance or a major rate retune is required, not just a latch at the failure threshold.
3. **Wear warning arrives late.** From fresh condition1 without maintenance, .40 is reached at tick100 and .30 crossed at117. Thirty consecutive worn samples finish at129, twelve ticks after service fails. A 30-tick voltage warning still precedes 120-tick crisis escalation, but it is already a warning about lost service rather than a pre-failure warning.
4. **Transit lost demand is not currently observable.** `Domain/Trips/ScheduleTripGenerator.cs:211` returns null for an unreachable nonlocal destination; GenerateTripsForTick records only an internal 60-tick retry cooldown. `TransitExecutionSystem.SubmitTrip` cancels route-null trips before retaining them. Counting ActiveTrips cannot detect three demanded unreachable trips: the very evidence was discarded. Add demand-attempt outcomes before route creation, not an observer over surviving trips.
5. **Completion observer currently misses most trips.** The callback in `TransitExecutionSystem.RecordOutsideArrivalCallback` is limited to outside Food/MoveOut transactions. It cannot implement routine-trip deadline success, delivery recovery or City Status cohorts. Preserve that financial callback; add a separate all-trip outcome port.
6. **Current queue fallback does not read current passenger wait.** TransitExecutionSystem compares trip.WaitTicks against15/5 while queued, but TripRecord wait is updated only on delivery/removal. New queued trips usually remain at zero despite ElevatorPassenger.WaitTicks increasing. Prequeue diversion at queue>=3 works; postqueue diversion should query live passenger wait directly and record exactly once. Existing behavior therefore cannot be used as evidence that every delayed Resident diverts after15 ticks.
7. **Existing wellbeing does not consume utilities.** `ResidentWellbeingSystem.Advance` service is `.80 * specialist service multiplier`, capped by household underprovision; no grid/water input. `ResidentNeedsSystem` replenishes home hygiene without checking water. Infrastructure failure is visible in projections but proposed live consequences need actual new integration.
