## Alternatives and selected scope

1. Global residents/totalcapacity factor: inexpensive but penalizes unrelated homes, conflates visitors and tenure, and rewards unused industrial/shaft capacity. Reject.
2. Home occupancy/capacity only: legal capacity gate makes overcrowding difficult/unattainable; misses small legal homes and actual shared-space peaks. Retain as diagnostic compliance, not sole welfare equation.
3. **Choose structural home space plus actual present-room excess**. Home floor area and household membership explain stable pressure; live local shared demand explains peaks. No new relocation/scheduling/admission/room-limit model in this run.
4. Noise full acoustic portal/path solver: potentially richer but portals have no acoustic state; adds unsupported mechanics/performance cost. Reject for this run.
5. **Choose existing bounded geometric activity-noise estimate moved into Domain**, preserve activity strengths/attenuation/Quiet Hours scope. One domain computation drives both wellbeing and overlay; no second acoustic calculation. Explicitly label estimated activity sound, not physics.

This run adds no non-occupant room-archetype noise: an empty workshop/pump remains known zero activity noise. New industrial equipment emissions require explicit units/state/source rules later. This restriction prevents inventing source strengths for existing content whose operation may be unavailable.
